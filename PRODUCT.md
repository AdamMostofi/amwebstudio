# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two distinct audiences:

**Business owners** (restaurants, cafes, clinics, shops, barbers in Beirut) — they're busy running their business. They've been told they "need a website" but find it complicated or expensive. They're on their phones between customers. They need to see that this is simple, affordable, and built for them — not some generic agency template.

**End customers** (people visiting the demo menu) — when a restaurant owner shows their friend or a customer scans a QR code, the site needs to impress. It must look fast, clean, and professional. The demo menu is a live proof point.

## Product Purpose

A.M Web Studio exists to get every Beirut business online with zero friction. The site itself must demonstrate the quality, speed, and simplicity we sell. If our site feels like a template, why would anyone trust us to build theirs?

## Brand Personality

Warm, confident, simple.

Warm — not cold corporate. This is Beirut. Personal, human.
Confident — not aggressive. We know what we're doing, and it shows.
Simple — not simplistic. Every element earns its place. No decoration for decoration's sake.

Tone of voice: direct, helpful, never hype. "We build websites" not "We revolutionize digital presence."

## Anti-references

- No template feel (Wix, Squarespace, generic WordPress themes). The site must feel custom-built, because it is.
- No stock photography of "business people shaking hands."
- No dark mode / SaaS-landing-page aesthetic (not a dev tool).
- No over-designed or artsy. Not a portfolio for a creative agency — a sales page for a service business.
- No hero-metric template ("X clients", "Y years", "Z dollars saved").
- No em dashes.

## Design Principles

**Practice what you preach.** The site must prove the quality we're selling. If it loads slow, looks generic, or feels off, we've lost before the pitch.

**Show, don't tell.** The live demo menu does more selling than any paragraph. Let the work speak.

**Zero complexity.** Every page or section answers one question. The pricing is on the page. The process is clear. Contact is one click.

**Mobile is the primary.** Beirut business owners live on WhatsApp and Instagram. The site must feel native on a phone — not just "responsive" but designed for thumbs first.

**Local confidence.** Proudly Beirut. Not hiding that we're a small studio — leaning into it. Local knowledge is a feature, not a limitation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AAA target. Specific requirements:
- High contrast ratios throughout (7:1 for normal text, 4.5:1 for large)
- No information conveyed by color alone
- Keyboard navigable
- Reduced motion respected
- Arabic language support consideration for future bilingual expansion
